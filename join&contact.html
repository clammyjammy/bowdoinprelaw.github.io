Join & Contact

Bowdoin Pre-Law Society aims to foster a space for everyone and anyone interested in the area of law to come together and learn collectively. We plan to connect current students with Bowdoin alumni or prominent figures who have experience working in the legal system, provide mentorship, and publish a Bowdoin Law Review for students to explore their passions within law and engage in legal writing.
